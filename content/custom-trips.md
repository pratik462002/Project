# Custom Trips
Headline: Tailor Your Dream Vacation
Description: Tell us where you want to go and what makes your ideal trip. Our destination specialists will craft a bespoke itinerary suited to your pace, preferences, and budget.
Available trip types:
- Family Holiday
- Romantic Honeymoon
- Adventure & Wildlife
- Luxury & Wellness
- Heritage & Culture
- Corporate / Group Offsite

Form fields:
- Full Name (required)
- Phone Number with Country Code (required)
- Email Address (required)
- Destination(s) of Interest (required)
- Approximate Travel Month / Dates (required)
- Duration (in Days / Nights)
- Number of Adults (required)
- Number of Children (under 12)
- Preferred Hotel Category (3-Star Comfort / 4-Star Premium / 5-Star Luxury)
- Budget Estimate per Person
- Special Requests (Food preferences, private vehicle, mobility assistance, celebrations)
