# Policies

## Booking Terms
- A token advance deposit of 25% to 50% (depending on destination and flight ticketing requirements) is required to confirm land package reservations.
- Airfares are subject to real-time airline rules and require 100% advance payment at the time of ticketing.
- Vouchers and final hotel confirmations are issued upon receipt of full payment 7 to 10 days before departure.

## Cancellation Policy
- 30 days or more prior to departure: 15% service charge of total land package cost.
- 29 to 15 days prior to departure: 30% of total package cost.
- 14 to 7 days prior to departure: 50% of total package cost.
- Within 7 days or No Show: 100% non-refundable.
- Flight ticket cancellations follow individual airline fare rules and cancellation fees.

## Privacy Policy
- InstaHoliday Solutions Pvt Ltd respects visitor confidentiality.
- All enquiry details (name, phone, email, travel dates) are strictly used by our internal travel advisors to prepare itineraries and provide tour customer service.
- We never sell, rent, or lease customer data to third-party marketing entities.
- Data retention: Enquiry records are archived after 12 months unless ongoing active reservations exist.

## Terms and Conditions
- Sightseeing schedules are subject to local weather conditions and governmental regulations.
- In unavoidable circumstances (force majeure, weather, road closures), equivalent alternative arrangements will be provided.
