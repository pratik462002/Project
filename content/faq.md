# FAQs

## Question
How do I book a tour package with InstaHoliday Solutions?
Answer:
You can browse our domestic or international packages, click "Enquire About This Package", or connect with us directly via WhatsApp or phone (+91 9822729072). Our travel specialist will discuss your dates, customize your itinerary, share the transparent quotation, and guide you through easy confirmation.

## Question
Can the itineraries be customized for private families or couples?
Answer:
Absolutely! Every package on our website can be customized. You can modify the duration, hotel tier, meal plan, sightseeing list, or vehicle type to suit your personal requirements.

## Question
What payment options are accepted?
Answer:
We accept NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI, and major credit/debit cards. Booking confirmation requires a nominal advance deposit, with the balance payable in convenient installments prior to departure.

## Question
Do you assist with Visas and International Travel Insurance?
Answer:
Yes. For all our international packages (Dubai, Europe, Vietnam, Sri Lanka, Thailand, Singapore, Bali), our dedicated visa desk assists you with document verification, appointment scheduling, application submission, and comprehensive overseas travel insurance.

## Question
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
Answer:
Cancellations made 30+ days prior to departure incur nominal processing charges. Full details are outlined in our verified booking terms and clearly communicated on your booking confirmation voucher.
