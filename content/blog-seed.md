## Content
Vietnam is rapidly becoming one of the most sought-after international holiday destinations for Indian travellers. With easy electronic visa processing, direct flights from major Indian hubs, delicious culinary diversity, and warm hospitality, it offers unmatched value.

### Key Highlights Not to Miss
1. **Halong Bay Overnight Cruise**: Sailing among the dramatic limestone pillars aboard a luxury wooden junk or modern steel cruise vessel is an experience of a lifetime. Make sure to choose a cruise that includes kayaking through Luon Cave.
2. **Hoi An Lantern Town**: In the evening, this UNESCO World Heritage town comes alive with thousands of glowing silk lanterns reflecting over the Thu Bon River.
3. **Ba Na Hills & Golden Bridge**: Take the world-record cable car up into the misty mountains of Da Nang and walk along the iconic bridge supported by two colossal stone hands.

### Practical Tips
- **Currency**: Vietnamese Dong (VND). Cash is widely used in local markets, while cards are accepted at all star-category hotels.
- **Best Season**: November to April offers dry, pleasant weather across both northern and central Vietnam.

## Content
Dubai effortlessly marries futuristic architectural ambition with timeless Arabian heritage. For families traveling with children and elders, Dubai provides pristine safety, world-class infrastructure, and non-stop entertainment.

### Top Family Experiences
1. **Burj Khalifa At the Top**: Soar to levels 124 & 125 in high-speed elevators and look down at the sprawling cityscape.
2. **Evening Desert Safari with BBQ**: Dune bashing in a 4x4 Land Cruiser, camel rides, falconry photo opportunities, and authentic live Tanoura dancing.
3. **Dubai Miracle Garden**: Over 150 million blooming flowers sculpted into life-sized airplanes, castles, and animal figures.
4. **Abu Dhabi Sheikh Zayed Mosque**: A serene architectural masterpiece of white marble, gold leaf, and crystal chandeliers.
