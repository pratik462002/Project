## Day-wise itinerary
### Day 1 — Arrival at Port Blair & Cellular Jail Light and Sound Show
Details: Arrive at Port Blair Airport where our tour executive greets you. Transfer to the hotel and check in. In the afternoon, visit the historic Cellular Jail followed by the stirring Light and Sound Show narrating India's freedom struggle.
Meals: Dinner
Overnight stay: Port Blair

### Day 2 — Port Blair to Havelock Island & Radhanagar Beach Sunset
Details: Board the high-speed ferry to Havelock Island. Check in to your beach resort. In the afternoon, visit Asia's renowned Radhanagar Beach (Beach No. 7). Enjoy the pristine sunset and leisurely beach walks.
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Overnight stay: Havelock Island

### Day 3 — Elephant Beach Water Sports Excursion
Details: Head to Elephant Beach via speed boat. Indulge in complimentary snorkeling or try jet skiing, scuba diving, and sea walking. Afternoon at leisure to explore local cafes.
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Overnight stay: Havelock Island

### Day 4 — Havelock to Neil Island (Shaheed Dweep) & Bharatpur Beach
Details: Ferry to Neil Island. Visit Bharatpur Beach renowned for vibrant coral reefs, and Laxmanpur Beach known for its spectacular sunset and Natural Howrah Bridge rock formation.
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Overnight stay: Neil Island

### Day 5 — Neil Island to Port Blair & Sagarika Emporium
Details: Return by morning ferry to Port Blair. Afternoon visit to Sagarika Cottage Industries Emporium and local markets for pearl jewelry and local handicraft souvenirs.
Meals: Breakfast, Dinner
Overnight stay: Port Blair

### Day 6 — Departure from Port Blair
Details: Breakfast at hotel. Timely transfer to Port Blair Airport for your flight back home with cherished island memories.
Meals: Breakfast
Overnight stay: None
